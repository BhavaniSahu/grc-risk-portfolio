Vulnerability-to-control mapping across ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF 2.0, and India's DPDP Act, 2023. Each row includes inherent vs. residual risk, the applicable controls, and a plain-language business translation suitable for non-technical stakeholders.

> All findings below are hypothetical/generalized examples built for self-directed GRC training and do not describe any real organization's environment.

| Annex A Control Gap (primary: A.8.16 — Monitoring Activities; secondary/compounding: A.8.17 — Clock Synchronization) | Broken SIEM Log Ingestion — App server logs stop reaching the SIEM | The App server logs stopped flowing due to overflow of disk storage, and there is no compensating control/alert present to identify the miss. The server handles sensitive data/credential data and is critical, the miss of logs is a risk in case of an intrusion and log activity would be missed, and also once it is fixed, clock out of sync highly impacts log correlation to identify the sequence of events during an investigation or audit. Incorrect correlation could result in missing few events although related. | High | High | A.8.16 (Monitoring Activities) — primary; A.8.17 (Clock Synchronization) — secondary/compounding | DE.CM-09 (Computing hardware/software, runtime environments, and their data are monitored) - primary, covers A.8.16; DE.AE-03 (Information is correlated from multiple sources) - secondary/compounding, covers A.8.17 | Section 8(5) - reasonable security safeguards (primary, monitoring blind spot delays/prevents breach detection); Section 8(6) - breach notification to Board/Data Principals (secondary - this finding doesn't cause a breach itself, but cripples the organization's ability to detect one has occurred, which directly undermines its ability to meet the 8(6) notification timeline if a breach happens elsewhere in the environment) | The App server stops sending logs to the event logging and alerting manager due to low disk storage, and this goes unnoticed because there is no mechanism in place to notify us when it happens.
